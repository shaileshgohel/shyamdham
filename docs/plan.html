શ્યામધામ વ્યવસ્થાપન પોર્ટલ

A plan for one cloud portal that handles members, messaging, accounts, donation receipts, wadi rental bills, inventory and user rights, with ten wireframes of the main screens.

What the portal covers

Each of your eight requirements becomes a module. They share one member list and one set of books, so a donation or a rental bill is entered once and shows up in the receipt, the WhatsApp message, the accounts and the audit log.

Wireframes

Low-detail layouts that show what sits on each screen, not final colours or styling. The interface is in Gujarati with an English switch. All names and amounts are sample data.

How it is built

Web app that works on phone and PC
Opens in any browser, installs to the phone home screen. No app store needed. Gujarati first, English switch.
Cloud hosting in India
Managed server and PostgreSQL database in a Mumbai data centre, automatic daily backups, HTTPS everywhere.
WhatsApp Business API
The official Meta API through an approved provider. Needs one dedicated number, business verification and pre-approved message templates.
PDF receipts and bills
Generated on the server with Gujarati fonts, your letterhead, running number per financial year and a verification QR code.
Login by mobile OTP
No passwords to share or forget. Rights are checked on the server for every action, not only hidden in the menu.
Tamper-evident records
Financial documents are never deleted or silently edited. Corrections are a cancel plus a new document, both logged.

Delivery in phases

About 15 weeks from kickoff to full go-live for a small team. The office can start using members and donation receipts from week 7, with username and password login and receipts printed or shared by hand. WhatsApp sending and OTP login are added in the last stage. These are planning estimates and will firm up after the first phase.

Week 1Finalise details

Collect receipt and bill formats, account heads, rate card, role list. Set up the domain, hosting and a test site.

Weeks 2–4Foundation and members

Username and password login, roles, audit log, member and family registration, directory, import of the existing member list from Excel.

Weeks 5–7Donations and accounts

Receipt entry, PDF to print or download, account heads, vouchers, day book and head-wise reports. First go-live.

Weeks 8–10Wadi booking and bills

Calendar, rate card, deposits, bill PDF to print or download, payment posting.

Weeks 11–12Inventory and reports

Item register, issue and return against bookings, monthly and yearly reports, Super Admin dashboard. WhatsApp number verification is applied for here.

Weeks 13–14WhatsApp, messaging and OTP

Automatic WhatsApp sending of receipts and bills, booking reminders, broadcast messages to members, and login by mobile OTP in place of passwords.

Week 15Training and handover

Opening balances, hands-on training for each role in Gujarati, a short user guide, full go-live and support.

Decisions needed from the committee

  • WhatsApp number. Which number becomes the official Shyamdham number? It cannot stay on the normal WhatsApp app once it moves to the API. Trust registration papers are needed for verification.
  • 80G status. If the trust has 80G approval, receipts must carry the approval number and the donor's PAN, and cash donations above ₹2,000 do not qualify for deduction. Please confirm the exact receipt wording with your CA.
  • GST on rentals. Whether wadi bills need GST depends on the trust's registration and turnover. This is a question for your CA, and the bill format follows the answer.
  • Account heads and opening balances. The list of income and expense heads in use today, and cash and bank balances on the start date.
  • Rate card. Charges for each facility, member and non-member rates, deposit rules and cancellation rules.
  • Who gets which role. Names and mobile numbers for each role, and who the Super Admin is.
  • Existing data. Is the member list in Excel, a register, or an old software? This decides the import effort.

Running costs to budget for. Hosting and database, the domain name, WhatsApp charges (Meta bills per message sent, and utility messages like receipts cost less than marketing broadcasts), SMS for OTP, and yearly support. I have not put rupee figures here because they depend on the provider you choose and your message volume.
